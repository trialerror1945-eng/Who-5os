========================================================================
DISEASE WITHOUT SYMPTOMS
========================================================================
Leg symptoms were recorded only in 2001-2004, so this analysis uses
the 3362 participants with both the symptom item and complete
predictors. It is a subgroup analysis, not the primary cohort.

--- PREVALENCE BY SYMPTOM STATUS ---
group                             n   cases   prevalence
Reports leg pain                367      87        23.7%
Reports no leg pain            2995     643        21.5%

  Cases occurring in people who report no leg pain: 643 of 730 (88.1%)
  Prevalence differs between the two groups by +2.2 percentage points.
  Asking about symptoms barely separates the groups at all.

--- 'EXAMINE WHOEVER COMPLAINS' AS A TRIAGE RULE ---
  sensitivity 0.119   specificity 0.894
  PPV 0.237   NPV 0.785
  examines 10.9% of attendees, finds 87 of 730 cases
  AUC of the symptom item alone: 0.506
  A rule that finds one case in eight is not a triage strategy.

--- THE SCORE, WHERE IT MATTERS ---
  AUC, all participants in this subgroup : 0.740
  AUC, restricted to those reporting no pain: 0.755  (n=2995, cases=643)
  Discrimination is undiminished in the silent majority, which is
  the group a symptom-led approach cannot reach by construction.

--- AT THE SAME EXAMINATION BURDEN AS THE SYMPTOM RULE ---
  Examining 367 people (10.9%):
    by symptoms        87 cases
    by triage score   201 cases  (+114, +131%)
